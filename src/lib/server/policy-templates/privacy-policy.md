## 2. Data Collected

{{companyName}} collects the following categories of personal data:

### 2.1 Account and Identity Data

- Name, email address, job title, company name
- Account credentials (passwords stored in hashed form only)
- Profile information provided by the user

### 2.2 Usage and Product Data

- Log data including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and feature usage

### 2.3 Payment and Billing Data

- Billing address and contact information

- Background check results (where legally permitted)

### 2.5 Data Provided by Customers

In the course of using {{companyName}}'s services, customers may upload or input data that contains personal information about their own users or employees. {{companyName}} processes this data as a data processor on behalf of the customer (data controller).

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

{{companyName}} processes personal data on the following legal bases under applicable privacy law (including GDPR where applicable):

- **Contract performance**: Processing necessary to provide the services described in our Terms of Service
- **Legitimate interests**: Processing for fraud prevention, security, product improvement, and direct marketing to existing customers, where our interests are not overridden by individual rights
- **Legal obligation**: Processing required to comply with applicable law (e.g., tax records, employment law)
- **Consent**: Where we rely on consent (e.g., marketing emails to non-customers), individuals may withdraw consent at any time

## 4. How Data Is Used

{{companyName}} uses personal data to:

- Provide, operate, and improve the {{companyName}} service
- Authenticate users and maintain account security
- Process payments and send billing communications
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Send product updates, security notices, and (where consented) marketing communications
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Meet legal and regulatory obligations
- Analyze usage patterns to improve product functionality

{{companyName}} does not sell personal data to third parties, and does not use customer data to train AI models without explicit written consent.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have the following rights regarding their personal data:

- **Right of access**: Obtain a copy of the personal data {{companyName}} holds about you
- **Right to rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to erasure**: Request deletion of personal data where there is no legal basis for continued processing
- **Right to restriction**: Request that processing be restricted in certain circumstances
- **Right to data portability**: Receive personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to withdraw consent**: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact {{companyName}} at the address in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days (or 72 hours for security-related requests where required by law).

## 6. Data Retention Periods

{{companyName}} retains personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Key retention periods:

- Account data: Retained for the duration of the account relationship plus 1 year
- Usage logs: 90 days online; 12 months archived
- Payment records: 7 years (tax and accounting requirements)
- Employee data: Duration of employment plus 7 years (varies by jurisdiction)
- Marketing contact data: 2 years from last meaningful interaction

For full details, see the Data Retention Policy.

## 7. Third-Party Sharing and Sub-Processors

{{companyName}} shares personal data with third-party service providers only as necessary to deliver our services. All sub-processors are subject to contractual data protection obligations at least as protective as those in this policy.

Key categories of sub-processors include:

- Cloud infrastructure providers (hosting and storage)
- Payment processor (billing)
- Analytics and monitoring tools
- Email and communication platforms
- Customer support tooling

A current list of sub-processors is available upon request. Customers are notified of material sub-processor changes with at least 30 days notice.

{{companyName}} does not transfer personal data to countries outside the EEA without ensuring adequate safeguards are in place (e.g., EU Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions).

## 8. Security Measures

{{companyName}} implements technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encryption of data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Access controls based on the principle of least privilege
- Multi-factor authentication for all internal system access
- Regular security assessments, penetration testing, and vulnerability management
- Security awareness training for all personnel with access to personal data
- Incident response procedures including data breach notification processes
