## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the rules and guidelines governing the appropriate use of {{companyName}}'s information systems, networks, devices, and data. Its purpose is to protect {{companyName}}, its employees, customers, and partners from harm caused by misuse of company resources, whether intentional or inadvertent.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and any other individuals granted access to {{companyName}} systems and resources, regardless of location. It covers all company-owned, leased, or managed devices and any personal devices used to access company resources (BYOD).

{{companyName}} operates a technology stack that includes: {{stack}}. All systems within this environment are subject to this policy.

## 3. Permitted Use of Company Systems and Data

Company systems, networks, and data are provided for the purpose of conducting {{companyName}} business. Authorized uses include:

- Performing assigned job responsibilities and duties
- Communicating with colleagues, customers, partners, and vendors on company business
- Accessing company data and applications necessary for job functions
- Limited, reasonable personal use that does not interfere with job performance, consume significant company resources, or violate any other provision of this policy

## 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on {{companyName}} systems and networks:

### 4.1 Security Violations
- Attempting to circumvent or disable security controls, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, or encryption
- Unauthorized scanning, probing, or testing of networks or systems without explicit written authorization
- Installing or executing malware, spyware, ransomware, keyloggers, or any other malicious software
- Sharing access credentials or using another person's credentials to access systems

### 4.2 Data Misuse
- Accessing, copying, modifying, or disclosing data without authorization or legitimate business need
- Transmitting confidential or restricted company data to personal email accounts or unauthorized external storage
- Using company data for personal gain or to benefit a competitor

### 4.3 Inappropriate Content
- Creating, accessing, storing, or transmitting content that is obscene, sexually explicit, harassing, discriminatory, threatening, or defamatory
- Accessing websites or services known to host illegal or harmful content
- Using company systems for gambling, operating a personal business, or political campaigning

### 4.4 Intellectual Property and Legal Violations
- Copying, distributing, or using software, media, or content in violation of applicable licenses or copyright law
- Using company systems to engage in activities that violate local, national, or international laws
- Reverse engineering proprietary software or systems without authorization

### 4.5 Resource Abuse
- Using company resources (bandwidth, compute, storage) for cryptocurrency mining or other unauthorized resource-intensive activities
- Intentionally degrading network or system performance

## 5. Internet and Email Usage

### 5.1 Internet Usage
Internet access is provided for business purposes. Users must not:

- Access or download content from sites known to host malware or illegal material
- Use anonymizing proxies or VPNs not approved by {{companyName}} to bypass security controls
- Stream non-business media in a manner that materially impacts network performance

### 5.2 Email Usage
Company email is the official communication channel of {{companyName}}. Users must:

- Not use company email addresses to register for personal services or non-business mailing lists
- Apply caution when opening attachments or clicking links, especially from unknown senders
- Report suspicious or phishing emails to the Security Team immediately
- Not auto-forward company email to personal or external accounts

## 6. Software Installation

Only approved, licensed software may be installed on company devices. Users must:

- Submit software installation requests through the designated IT/Security approval process
- Not install browser extensions, plugins, or add-ons without IT approval
- Not install peer-to-peer file-sharing applications or unapproved remote access tools
- Keep all authorized software up to date with security patches

## 7. Monitoring and Privacy Notice

{{companyName}} reserves the right to monitor, audit, and log all activity on company-owned systems, networks, and devices to the extent permitted by applicable law. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company resources. Monitoring may occur without prior notice and is conducted for security, compliance, legal, and operational purposes.

Use of company systems constitutes consent to such monitoring.

## 8. Reporting Security Violations

All employees and contractors are required to report suspected policy violations, security incidents, or suspicious activity promptly. Reports should be directed to:

- **Security Team**: security@{{companyName}}.com (or the designated incident reporting channel)
- Reports may be made anonymously where local law permits

Retaliation against anyone who reports a security concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## 9. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Violation of this policy may result in:

- Immediate revocation of system access
- Disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract
- Legal action where violations constitute criminal activity
- Civil liability for damages caused to {{companyName}} or third parties

The severity of the response will be proportional to the nature and impact of the violation.

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed annually or upon significant changes to {{companyName}}'s technology environment or applicable laws and regulations.
