## 1. Purpose

This Security Policy establishes the guidelines and requirements for protecting {{companyName}}'s information assets, systems, and data from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access {{companyName}}'s systems and data.

## 3. Information Security Principles

- **Confidentiality**: Information is accessible only to those authorized to access it
- **Integrity**: Information is accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification
- **Availability**: Information is accessible and usable upon demand by authorized users

## 4. Access Control

All access to {{companyName}} systems must be granted based on the principle of least privilege. Access rights are reviewed quarterly and revoked upon termination.

## 5. Technology Stack

{{companyName}} uses the following technologies: {{stack}}. Security controls are implemented at each layer of the stack.

## 6. Incident Response

Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours to the security team. All incidents are logged, investigated, and remediated according to the Incident Response Policy.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed annually or upon significant changes to {{companyName}}'s technology or business operations.
