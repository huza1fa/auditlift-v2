## 1. Purpose

This Physical Security Policy establishes requirements for protecting {{companyName}}'s physical facilities, equipment, and assets from unauthorized access, theft, damage, or interference. Physical security is a foundational layer of {{companyName}}'s overall security posture — a physical breach can render technical controls ineffective.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all {{companyName}} office locations, co-working spaces used for company work, data center facilities (owned or collocated), and remote work environments. It applies to all employees, contractors, visitors, and vendors who access any {{companyName}} physical facility or equipment.

## 3. Facility Access Controls

### 3.1 Physical Access Mechanisms

{{companyName}} implements layered physical access controls at all facilities:

- **Perimeter controls**: All entry points to {{companyName}} facilities are secured with electronic access control systems (badge/key fob or equivalent)
- **Server/network rooms**: Restricted to authorized IT and Security personnel only; access is controlled by a separate credential from general office access
- **Key management**: Physical keys and access cards are tracked in an inventory log; lost or stolen keys/cards must be reported and deactivated immediately

### 3.2 Access Provisioning and Revocation

- Physical access is provisioned as part of the onboarding process based on job role and least-privilege principles
- Access rights are reviewed quarterly; any access no longer required by job function is revoked
- Physical access for terminated or transferred employees is revoked on the same day as logical access revocation

### 3.3 Access Logging and Review

All physical access to secured areas is logged electronically. Access logs are reviewed monthly by the Security Team for anomalous patterns (e.g., off-hours access, repeated failed attempts). Logs are retained for a minimum of 12 months.

## 4. Visitor Management

### 4.1 Visitor Registration

All visitors to {{companyName}} facilities must:

- Sign in at reception or via the visitor management system, providing name, organization, and purpose of visit
- Present government-issued photo identification upon request
- Receive a visitor badge that is visibly displayed throughout their visit

### 4.2 Escort Requirements

Visitors must be escorted by an authorized employee at all times in non-public areas. Visitors must not be left unattended in areas containing company equipment, servers, or confidential materials.

### 4.3 Visitor Log Retention

Visitor logs are retained for a minimum of 12 months and are available for audit review.

## 5. Clean Desk and Clear Screen Policy

### 5.1 Clean Desk

All employees and contractors must:

- Clear desks of sensitive materials (printed documents, physical media, notes containing credentials) at the end of each workday and whenever leaving their workspace unattended for more than 15 minutes
- Store sensitive physical documents in locked drawers or cabinets when not in active use
- Shred or securely dispose of printed confidential materials when no longer needed; do not place in open recycling bins

### 5.2 Clear Screen

- Computer screens must be locked when a workstation is left unattended for any period
- Automatic screen lock must be configured to activate after no more than 5 minutes of inactivity
- Screens should be positioned to minimize visibility to visitors or passersby (privacy screen filters are provided on request)

## 6. Media and Equipment Handling

### 6.1 Asset Inventory

All company-owned equipment (laptops, servers, mobile devices, external storage) is tracked in an asset inventory. Each asset record includes the assigned user, serial number, and physical location.

### 6.2 Removal of Equipment

Removing company equipment from {{companyName}} premises requires manager approval. Employees regularly working remotely may be pre-authorized for equipment removal. Unauthorized removal of company assets is a policy violation.

### 6.3 Physical Media

- USB drives and external storage devices must be company-issued, encrypted, and approved by IT
- Personal USB drives and unauthorized removable media are prohibited on company systems
- Physical media containing sensitive data must be stored securely and tracked in the asset inventory

## 7. Equipment Disposal

All equipment decommissioned or disposed of must undergo secure data erasure before disposal or repurposing:

- **Hard drives and SSDs**: Overwritten using a NIST 800-88-compliant method (Purge or Clear, depending on classification) or physically destroyed
- **Mobile devices**: Factory reset with remote wipe confirmation
- **Servers**: Drives removed and destroyed separately from chassis disposal

A certificate of destruction or disposal record must be obtained and retained for each asset disposed of. Equipment must never be discarded in general waste or donated without verified data erasure.

## 8. CCTV and Physical Monitoring

Where applicable and permitted by local law, {{companyName}} may operate CCTV or video surveillance at facility entry points and high-security areas. CCTV footage is:

- Retained for a minimum of 30 days
- Accessible only to authorized Security and Facilities personnel
- Used solely for security investigation purposes

Employees are notified of CCTV coverage through workplace notices as required by applicable law.

## 9. Remote Office and Home Office Physical Security

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining adequate physical security at their work location:

- Work devices must be secured when unattended and never left in visible sight in an unattended vehicle
- Sensitive conversations or screen content should not be visible or audible to household members or the public
- Employees must apply the clean desk and clear screen standards in Section 5 to their home office
- Physical documents containing {{companyName}} confidential data must be stored securely and shredded when no longer needed; home shredders or secure document disposal services should be used

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed annually or following any physical security incident, significant change in facility usage, or audit finding.
